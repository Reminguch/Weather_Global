4 October 2026 · Original GC · MSE · AMSE λ=0.1 · Full AMSE

Full AMSE restores substantially more spectral power, while MSE and AMSE λ=0.1 preserve more deterministic forecast skill. These are completed SWA evaluations; the report contains no new inference.

ModelMean relative power error
Original GC48.94%

Equal weighting of 11 spectral fields, k=1–180, and days 1, 3, 7, 10. Eight exploratory 2023 starts; raw powers are pooled before ratios. Global RMSE and exact GraphCast loss have different domains and weights. The 32-start 2022 evaluation is reported separately.

One fixed Original GC and one fixed MSE reference are shown. Repeatability and paired-baseline comparisons are documented in the PDF and data audit. Wind-speed global RMSE was not saved; WS10 and WS850 have spectral results only.
