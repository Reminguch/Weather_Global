## Recorded quantities

The evaluator's optional prediction observer receives the physical FP32 ERA5 truth, independent GraphCast forecast, and corrected forecast already used by the exact loss calculation. It exports nine primitive global fields at every lead: T2m, U10, V10, MSLP, TP6, T850, Q700, U850 and V850. Wind speeds WS10 and WS850 are derived from their physical vector components before the transform.

For every initialization, lead and variable, it retains forecast/truth power, real cross-power and error power. The transform is a forward-normalized real longitude FFT, with positive-frequency power doubled except the even-grid Nyquist mode. Each latitude's zonal mean is removed; the zero mode is retained as zero in the files and omitted in figures. The reported spectrum averages latitudes 30–60 degrees in both hemispheres using normalized cosine weights. Its mode number counts cycles around a latitude circle and has no unique latitude-independent wavelength. The sum of its power equals the weighted zonal-anomaly variance. These scalar spectra are not kinetic-energy spectra.

The report pools powers across initializations before calculating power ratios, normalized spectral error and signed spectral correlation. ERA5 spectra must agree exactly across all model runs. Repeated baseline spectra must satisfy the frozen `rtol=1e-3, atol=1e-10` check; exact baseline losses use `rtol=1e-3, atol=1e-6`. All reported baseline curves and exact-loss denominators use M1's canonical baseline after those checks. These tolerances are engineering checks, not scientific noninferiority margins.
