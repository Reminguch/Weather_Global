plots (former v5 Figures 14 and 15; v4 Figures 16 and 17). Their PDFs and original
figure blocks are retained here as historical material. The remaining amplitude
ratio and coherence plots stay in the manuscript. No evaluations were rerun.

literal former figure numbers. The clean copy excludes them. The common-grid
RMSE comparison and city examples become v5 Figures 14 and 15; their newly named
active PDFs are byte-identical copies of the former Figures 16 and 17.

they are not standalone documents or inputs to the active manuscript.
