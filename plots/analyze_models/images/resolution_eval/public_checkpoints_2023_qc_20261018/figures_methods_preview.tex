% Replacement Appendix C methods paragraphs:
We evaluate the principal M1 correction, its public $1^\circ$ GraphCast_small
backbone, and the public $0.25^\circ$ GraphCast checkpoint by generating
forecasts locally. The public checkpoints differ in mesh refinement
(levels 5 and 6), pressure levels (13 and 37), and training coverage
(1979--2015 and 1979--2017), as well as resolution. M1 is the same selected
seven-year correction used in the principal evaluation. Each forecast starts
from two observed states six hours apart and subsequently feeds back its own
predictions; M1's recurrent state starts at zero for each forecast.
Network calculations use bfloat16 with float32 physical fields. Checkpoint
checksums, source snapshots, normalization identities and initialization dates
are retained with the evaluation artifacts. The prior numerical-pilot exposure
and sensitivity excluding its three sampled initializations are disclosed
alongside the principal evaluation.

Forecasts and native $0.25^\circ$ ARCO ERA5 verification fields are remapped
to the same $1.5^\circ$ grid using first-order conservative spherical-cell
overlap with periodic longitude. For each diagnostic and lead, spatial squared
errors use normalized cosine-latitude weights; squared errors are then averaged
over 121--122 matched valid samples per lead and diagnostic before taking the
square root. The plotted
values are physical-unit RMSE and use no archived GraphCast forecast reference
or ENS normalization.

Paired uncertainty estimates use 10,000 non-wrapping block-bootstrap draws with
five consecutive initialization dates per block. Three- and seven-start block
lengths and the prior-pilot sensitivity are retained in the accompanying
results. Intervals are pointwise and conditional on the fixed checkpoints and
evaluation sample; they do not measure training-seed variability.
