Every surface/atmospheric-variable group has both _5d and _10d PDF/PNG files.

best_worst_* shows the exact three lowest and three highest M1 equal-degree
power mismatch scores across all 83 channels. These are relative ranks; inspect
the curves. Rankings are computed separately at each lead, so selected
best/worst fields can differ between days 5 and 10. Use familiar_* or the
variable-group plots to compare the same fields across leads.
Panel labels compare M1 and raw GraphCast using the same
equal-degree power mismatch: 100 mean_l |Pforecast/PERA5 - 1|, l=1..179.
Each model is explicitly labelled; smaller is closer to ERA5. Energy-weighted
scores remain available in the numeric table. Equal-degree mismatch emphasizes
weak spectral tails; energy-weighted mismatch emphasizes scales with most power.

At day 5, T2m preserves power relatively well. Z500/T850 retain most large-scale power
but lose small-scale power. Precipitation and vertical pressure velocity at
500 hPa have strong power deficits. Raw GraphCast is closer to ERA5 power than
M1 for most channels; power fidelity and forecast MSE are different quantities.

Fields: T=temperature, Z=geopotential, Q=specific humidity, U/V=horizontal wind
components, W=vertical pressure velocity, MSLP=mean sea-level pressure,
TP6h=6-hour precipitation. Numbers on atmospheric labels are pressure in hPa.

All panels include raw GC, raw M1, optimally filtered GC and raw matched
reset/reset on January 1, April 1, July 3 and October 1, 2023. Fixed checkpoints,
no uncertainty bands; no new forecasts or model selection in this analysis.
