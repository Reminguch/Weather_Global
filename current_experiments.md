### Split-AMSE failure diagnosis — 2026-10-17

- **Observed:** both arms are residual width512, differing in temporal inner dimension16/32. Relative to each starting checkpoint, final exact32 pooled GC loss increases **53.37%/62.30%**; even the first six-hour lead worsens **11.84%/20.78%**. All11 physical variables worsen. Step500 is worse than step2000, so the run partially recovers without recovering its parent skill. This is finite forecast degradation, separate from the final report's truth-power equality failure.
- **Spectral evidence:** from the saved eight2023 development starts, averaging per-start physical spherical diagnostics over days1/3/7/10 and positive degrees, precipitation amplitude mismatch decreases **90.18%/86.21%**, but the pattern component increases **41.86%/50.42%** and mean coherence decreases **0.122/0.129**. Forecast/truth total power rises **0.330→1.088 /0.337→1.172**. Pattern error increases across all83 saved spherical channels. These physical diagnostics are not the normalized, weighted training objectives. Source: the campaign's `evaluation/results/{di16,di32}_step{00000,02000}/spectral/spherical/*.npz`.
- **Training evidence:** compare complete104-record epochs0 and18 in each `runs/*/train_metrics.jsonl`. Mean GC loss rises **3.635→5.478 /3.639→5.852**; amplitude falls **0.512→0.198 /0.512→0.207**; pattern rises **3.986→5.489 /3.989→5.853**. Gradient cosine becomes **−0.538→−0.942 /−0.507→−0.922**. Late amplitude coefficients around0.87/0.90 do not mean90% of the actual update is amplitude: weighted raw gradient norms are nearly balanced and oppose strongly. Deterioration becomes pronounced around updates209–416. All2,000 updates per arm are finite, pass directional checks, and neither stall nor clip.
- **Established implementation limits:** the optimized pair is amplitude A and pattern C at lambda1; exact GC MSE is diagnostic only. A+C is AMSE, not MSE. `descent_valid` checks local gradient–displacement products on a trajectory with stopped weather feedback; it does not reevaluate post-update losses or guarantee improvement after generating a new closed-loop forecast. There is no line search or forecast-skill gate, and inline validation is disabled. Both terms share the same frozen scale4.541075928. Six audited modules match the frozen source;63 existing split-loss/optimizer/spherical tests pass. No concrete sign, routing, normalization, or duplicate-optimizer bug was found. [Objective/update](src/models/mamba/v24_Ilya/training/mgda_step.py), [spectral formula](src/models/mamba/v24_Ilya/training/spectral_loss.py), [MGDA optimizer](src/models/mamba/v24_Ilya/training/mgda_optimizer.py).
- **Interpretation and limits:** the evidence supports restoration of amplitude at the expense of correct patterns and grid accuracy. Objective mismatch explains why MGDA does not protect GC skill, but does not alone explain why the optimized pattern term also grows. Conflicting gradients, finite update size and stopped-feedback rollout dynamics are plausible contributors requiring a controlled replay. Di32's40.4% restart second-moment drift remains an additional numerical caveat; corresponding qualification GC-loss drift was0.197%, and di16 also fails with much smaller moment drift. The parent optimizer changes from AdamW to custom RMS along with the objective, so this is not an isolated loss-only ablation.
- **Proposed next diagnostic, not launched:** replay the same checkpoint/batch/update at original,0.1× and0.01× step sizes; measure A/C/GC before and after on both the fixed weather tape and a regenerated closed-loop trajectory. This separates finite-step failure from feedback sensitivity before another full run. Only this existing note was updated; training code, report tolerances and jobs remain unchanged.
